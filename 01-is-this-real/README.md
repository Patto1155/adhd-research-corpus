# 01 Is this real?

This folder answers whether ADHD is a real condition, how it is diagnosed, and why the number of diagnoses has risen.

- [What ADHD is](what-adhd-is.md): a plain definition, why it is dimensional, and what the international consensus says.
- [How diagnosis works](how-diagnosis-works.md): the criteria, why no scan or blood test exists, screeners versus diagnosis, and differences in who gets diagnosed.
- [More diagnoses than ever](more-diagnoses-than-ever.md): stable true prevalence, rising diagnoses, the relative-age effect, and the overdiagnosis debate.
- [Diagnosed as an adult](diagnosed-as-an-adult.md): how childhood ADHD is missed, how it changes with age, and the late-onset debate.
- [Women and girls](women-and-girls.md): later diagnosis, similar severity on thorough assessment, masking, and the thin hormone evidence.
