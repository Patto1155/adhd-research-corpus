# What's happening in my brain?

This folder covers what genetics, brain imaging and brain chemistry show about ADHD, how large the differences really are, and why "is my brain broken?" has a clear answer: no.

- [Is ADHD genetic, and will my children have it?](genes-and-inheritance.md): heritability, family risk by type of relative, the genome studies, and why there is no genetic test.
- [Is my brain damaged, smaller or "three years behind"?](brain-structure-and-development.md): the large brain-scan studies, how small and child-specific the differences are, and where "three years behind" comes from.
- [Is ADHD caused by low dopamine?](dopamine-noradrenaline-and-myths.md): what imaging, genetics and medicine response do and do not show about dopamine and noradrenaline.
- [Why is my ADHD so different from someone else's?](why-no-two-adhd-brains-are-alike.md): why ADHD has many routes and no single brain or test signature.
