# What is different about my brain?

What genetics, brain imaging and brain chemistry show about ADHD, and why "is my brain broken?" has a clear answer: no.

- [Is ADHD genetic, and will my children have it?](genes-and-inheritance.md): heritability, family risk by relative, genome studies, no genetic test.
- [Does ADHD mean my brain is damaged or "three years behind"?](brain-structure-and-development.md): large scan studies, how small the differences are, "three years behind".
- [Is ADHD caused by low dopamine?](dopamine-noradrenaline-and-myths.md): what imaging, genetics and medicine response show about brain chemistry.
- [Why is my ADHD so different from someone else's?](why-no-two-adhd-brains-are-alike.md): many routes to ADHD, and no single brain or test signature.
