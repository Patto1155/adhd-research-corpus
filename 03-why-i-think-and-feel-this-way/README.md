# Why do I think and feel this way?

This folder links everyday ADHD experiences (forgetting, lateness, uneven focus, intense emotions, late nights) to what research measures, and says where popular descriptions run ahead of the evidence.

- [Why can't I plan, remember what I was doing, or just get started?](executive-function-and-working-memory.md): executive function and working memory, and why lab tests and daily life disagree.
- [Why do I lose track of time and find waiting so hard?](time-waiting-and-rewards.md): time perception, preferring rewards now over later, and "time blindness" reconsidered.
- [If I have an attention deficit, why can I focus for hours on some things?](attention-regulation-and-hyperfocus.md): variable attention, and how thin the hyperfocus research is.
- [Why are my emotions so intense, and why does rejection hurt so much?](emotions-and-rejection.md): well-supported emotion dysregulation, and rejection sensitive dysphoria as an unvalidated term.
- [Why can't I fall asleep, and could poor sleep be what is wrong with me?](sleep-and-the-body-clock.md): later body clocks, sleep problems, and sleep conditions that look like ADHD.
