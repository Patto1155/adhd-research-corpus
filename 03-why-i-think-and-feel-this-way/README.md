These pages link everyday ADHD experiences, such as forgetting, lateness, uneven focus, intense emotions and late nights, to what research measures.

- [Executive function and ADHD: why can't I plan or get started?](executive-function-and-working-memory.md): planning and working memory, and why tests and daily life disagree.

- [ADHD and hyperfocus: why can I focus for hours on some things?](attention-regulation-and-hyperfocus.md): variable attention, and how thin the hyperfocus research is.
- [Emotions and rejection in ADHD: why do they hit so hard?](emotions-and-rejection.md): emotion dysregulation, and why rejection sensitive dysphoria is not yet established.
- [Sleep and ADHD: why can't I fall asleep, and is sleep the cause?](sleep-and-the-body-clock.md): later body clocks, sleep problems, and sleep conditions that resemble ADHD.
