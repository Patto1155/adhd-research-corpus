# 07 Family, work and other people

This folder answers what the research says about ADHD in life with other people: children and parents, partners, work and study, and how others see ADHD and whether to tell them.

- [ADHD in children and teenagers](adhd-in-children-and-teenagers.md): how common it is, why parenting is not the cause, and how symptoms change through adolescence.
- [Children and medication](children-and-medication.md): what is specific to children, including the MTA trial and follow-up, growth, and preschool use.
- [Parenting and what helps](parenting-and-what-helps.md): parent training and school supports, with the gap between parent-rated and blinded results.
- [When your partner has ADHD](when-your-partner-has-adhd.md): the thin research on adult relationships and what it does and does not show.
- [Work and study](work-and-study.md): income, unemployment and university outcomes on average, what registry data say about medication, and the thin evidence on adjustments.
- [Stigma and telling people](stigma-and-telling-people.md): how common negative beliefs about ADHD are, what self-stigma goes with, and the small research on disclosing to employers, friends and family.
