# ADHD and other people

What research says about ADHD in children and teenagers, parenting and medication for children, adult relationships, work and study, and stigma and telling people.

- [ADHD in children: how common, did we cause it, and what comes next?](adhd-in-children-and-teenagers.md): how common, why parenting does not appear to be the cause, how symptoms change.

- [Parent training and school support for ADHD: do they help?](parenting-and-what-helps.md): parent training and school supports, parent-rated versus blinded results.
