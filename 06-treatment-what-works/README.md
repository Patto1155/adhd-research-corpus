# 06 Treatment: what works

This folder answers what ADHD treatments do, how well they work, what the long-term safety data show, and what is still unknown. It describes evidence only; decisions about starting, stopping or changing any treatment belong with a prescriber (see [not medical advice](../08-reading-this-well/not-medical-advice.md)).

- [How ADHD medicines work](how-adhd-medicines-work.md): what stimulants and non-stimulants do in the brain, and why a good response does not prove a diagnosis.
- [How well medication works](how-well-medication-works.md): effect sizes in children and adults, why Cochrane and other reviewers grade the same trials differently, and the quality-of-life gap.
- [Medication safety](medication-safety.md): common side effects, blood pressure and long-term heart risk, growth, psychosis and misuse, with absolute figures where available.
- [Will medication change me?](will-medication-change-me.md): emotions, blunting, personality and identity, and how little is known about how often blunting happens.
- [What registries show](what-registries-show.md): crime, crashes, substance problems, injuries and suicidal behaviour in the same people on and off medication, and why that design is strong but not proof.
- [Staying on or stopping medication](staying-on-or-stopping-medication.md): discontinuation trials, the MTA long-term follow-up and what guidelines say about regular review.
- [Pregnancy and breastfeeding](pregnancy-and-breastfeeding.md): Nordic and US registry findings on stimulant exposure in pregnancy, absolute risks, and breastfeeding data.
- [Therapy and skills](therapy-and-skills.md): CBT, parent training, cognitive training, coaching and psychoeducation.
- [Lifestyle and other approaches](lifestyle-and-other-approaches.md): neurofeedback, diet, supplements, exercise and mindfulness, and how blinded ratings change the picture.
