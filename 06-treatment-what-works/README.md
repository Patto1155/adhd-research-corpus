# Which ADHD treatments work, and are they safe?

What ADHD treatments do, how well they work, what the safety data show, and what is still unknown. These pages describe evidence only; decisions about starting, stopping or changing any treatment belong with a prescriber (see [not medical advice](../08-reading-this-well/not-medical-advice.md)).

- [How do ADHD medicines work?](how-adhd-medicines-work.md): stimulants, non-stimulants, and why response does not prove ADHD.
- [How well does ADHD medication work?](how-well-medication-works.md): effect sizes, the Cochrane disagreement, the quality-of-life gap.
- [Is ADHD medication safe? Heart, growth, psychosis and misuse](medication-safety.md): side effects and long-term risks, with absolute figures where available.
- [Will ADHD medication change my personality or make me a zombie?](will-medication-change-me.md): emotions, blunting, identity, and what is not known.
- [Real-life outcomes on ADHD medication: what do registries show?](what-registries-show.md): crime, crashes, injuries and suicidal behaviour on and off medication.
- [Stopping ADHD medication: do benefits fade, and is it for life?](staying-on-or-stopping-medication.md): discontinuation trials, the MTA follow-up, regular review.
- [Pregnancy, breastfeeding and ADHD medication: is it safe?](pregnancy-and-breastfeeding.md): registry findings, absolute risks, breastfeeding data.
- [Therapy, coaching and skills training: do they work for ADHD?](therapy-and-skills.md): CBT, parent training, brain training, coaching.
- [Neurofeedback, diet, supplements, exercise: do they help ADHD?](lifestyle-and-other-approaches.md): what is left once raters are blinded.
