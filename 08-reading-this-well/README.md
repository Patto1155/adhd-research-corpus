# 08 Reading this well

This folder explains how to read the rest of the corpus: what the numbers mean, where the evidence comes from, and what these pages cannot do for you.

- [How to read the evidence](how-to-read-the-evidence.md): effect sizes and overlap, heritability, relative and absolute risk, sibling and within-person designs, blinding, and the evidence labels, with worked ADHD examples.
- [Myths and corrections](myths-and-corrections.md): common claims about ADHD, what the evidence supports, and where to read more.
- [Not medical advice](not-medical-advice.md): what this corpus cannot do, when to see a clinician, and crisis contacts.
- [Sources and guidelines](sources-and-guidelines.md): the guidelines and consensus statements behind the corpus, how sources were chosen, and the limits on checking them.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): technical terms from across the corpus, A to Z.
